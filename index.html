// about me

who is ana ?

Hi, I'm Ana — a Computer Science student who enjoys turning ideas into something visual and interactive. I'm currently learning web development, especially HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. What I enjoy most is seeing how a simple structure can slowly become an interface that feels alive — functional, but still has a little personality. Outside of coding, I'm interested in design, psychology, beautiful words, and anything that lets me explore how people think and connect. I tend to pay attention to little details, which is probably why I can spend way too long choosing colors, fonts, and tiny visual elements. I'm still learning and experimenting, but that's what makes building things fun. Every project is another little step in figuring out what kind of developer — and person — I want to become.
